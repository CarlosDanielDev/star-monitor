## ADR stub

Two files, not one: the template is copy-paste material (lean, no prose), the playbook is the reasoning (prose, no skeleton). Merging them forces every user to scroll rules to reach the skeleton — and the corpus shows the top repos front-load the artifact (title → badges → one-paragraph pitch; see {{L2}}).

- Badge-generator tooling — the template shows the pattern, not a generator.
